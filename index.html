Tools

My Creative Toolkit

The essential tools that power every project from concept to completion.

Godot Engine

The primary game development engine. Used for building 2D games with GDScript—from prototyping to polished releases. Chosen for its lightweight nature, open-source philosophy, and excellent 2D capabilities.

Aseprite

The go-to tool for pixel art creation and animation. Used to craft every sprite, tileset, character design, and environment asset with precision and artistic flair.

FL Studio

The music production workstation. Used for composing original soundtracks, designing game audio, and creating ambient tracks that bring projects to life with immersive soundscapes.

DaVinci Resolve

The professional video editing and color grading suite. Used for creating game trailers, showreels, motion graphics, and cinematic video content with industry-standard quality.
